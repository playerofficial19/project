Tired after work? Your dog still needs a walk.

Happy Paws is a small dog walking service in Vaasa run by Anna Lindqvist. We give your dog fresh air, exercise and company on the days when your calendar simply does not allow it.

Why families in Vaasa choose us

🐾 Insured and reliable

Every walk is covered by our liability insurance and you get a message with a photo when the walk is finished.

⏰ Flexible hours

Walks Monday to Saturday between 08:00 and 18:00. Book a one-time walk or a regular weekly slot - whatever fits your life.

🐕 Small groups

Maximum three friendly dogs per walk, so every dog gets real attention instead of just a hurried lap around the block.

Walk of the week

The walk of the week is picked from our daily walking photos. Customers receive these photos directly, and the best ones end up here and on our Instagram. It is one small way of showing that the walks really happen - and that the dogs really smile.

Curious about prices?

A single 45-minute walk costs 18 €, and regular customers get cheaper package prices. Read more on the services and prices page.
