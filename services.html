Services & prices

Every dog is different, so we offer more than one kind of walk. All services include a photo report and fresh water after the visit. Prices include VAT.

Group walk

A 45-minute walk in a small group of max three friendly dogs. Good for social dogs who love company.

Solo walk

A 45-minute one-on-one walk for dogs that prefer humans to other dogs, or are still learning their manners.

Puppy visit

A 30-minute home visit for puppies: play, a short walk, feeding and cleaning up little accidents.

Pet taxi

Need to get your dog to the vet or groomer? We drive your dog and wait with it if you cannot come along.
